Problem Statement: Unique Product Codes

Problem Description:

In a retail company, each product is assigned a unique code to avoid confusion and ensure accurate inventory management. An isogram is a word or phrase without a repeating letter. The company wants to ensure that all product codes are isograms to maintain uniqueness and prevent errors.

Given a string representing a product code, your task is to determine if it is an isogram. This will help the company verify the uniqueness of their product codes.

Input:

A single line containing a string (S) (1 ≤ |S| ≤ 100), where |S| is the length of the string. The string may contain letters, digits, and special characters.
Output:

Print "Yes" if the string is an isogram.
Print "No" otherwise.
Example:

Input:
Product123

Output:
Yes
Input:
Inventory2024

Output:
No
Explanation:

In the first example, "Product123" is an isogram because no characters repeat.
In the second example, "Inventory2024" is not an isogram because the character '2' repeats.
Note:

Ensure your solution handles all types of characters efficiently and can process the upper limits of the input constraints.
