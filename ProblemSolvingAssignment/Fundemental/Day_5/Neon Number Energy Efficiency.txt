Problem Statement: Neon Number Energy Efficiency

Problem Description:

In an effort to promote energy efficiency, a city council is evaluating the energy consumption of various streetlights. They have discovered that certain streetlights, identified by neon numbers, are more energy-efficient. A neon number is a number where the sum of the digits of its square is equal to the number itself. For example, 9 is a neon number because (9^2 = 81) and (8 + 1 = 9).

Given a range of integers, your task is to identify all neon numbers within that range. This will help the city council in identifying and promoting the use of energy-efficient streetlights.

Input:

The first line contains an integer (L) (1 ≤ (L) ≤ (10^6)), representing the lower bound of the range.
The second line contains an integer (R) (1 ≤ (R) ≤ (10^6)), representing the upper bound of the range.
Output:

Print all neon numbers within the range ([L, R]). Each number should be printed on a new line.
If there are no neon numbers in the given range, print "No neon numbers found".
Example:

Input:
1
100

Output:
1
9
Explanation:

Within the range 1 to 100, the neon numbers are 1 and 9.
Note:

Ensure your solution is efficient and can handle the upper limits of the input constraints.
