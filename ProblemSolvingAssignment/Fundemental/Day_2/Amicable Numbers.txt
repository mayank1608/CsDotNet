Problem Statement: Amicable Numbers

Problem Description:

Two numbers are considered amicable if each number is the sum of the proper divisors of the other. Proper divisors of a number are those divisors excluding the number itself.

Given two integers (a) and (b), your task is to determine if they are amicable numbers.

Input:

The first line contains an integer (a) (1 ≤ (a) ≤ (10^6)).
The second line contains an integer (b) (1 ≤ (b) ≤ (10^6)).
Output:

Print "Yes" if (a) and (b) are amicable numbers.
Print "No" otherwise.
Example:

Input:
220
284

Output:
Yes
Explanation:

The proper divisors of 220 are 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 11, 20, 22, 44, 55, and 110. Their sum is 284.
The proper divisors of 284 are 1, 2, 4, 71, and 142. Their sum is 220.
Since each number is the sum of the proper divisors of the other, 220 and 284 are amicable numbers.

Ensure your solution is efficient and can handle the upper limits of the input constraints.
