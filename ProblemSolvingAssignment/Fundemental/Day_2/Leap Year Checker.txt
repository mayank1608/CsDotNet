Problem Statement: Leap Year Checker
Problem Description:

Write a program to determine if a given year is a leap year. A leap year is exactly divisible by 4, except for years that are exactly divisible by 100. However, years that are exactly divisible by 400 are also leap years.

Input:

A single integer, ( Y ) (1 ≤ ( Y ) ≤ 10,000), representing the year.
Output:

Print "YES" if the year is a leap year.
Print "NO" if the year is not a leap year.
Example:

Input:
2000

Output:
YES
Input:
1900

Output:
NO
Explanation:

The year 2000 is a leap year because it is divisible by 400.
The year 1900 is not a leap year because it is divisible by 100 but not by 400.
Constraints:

The input year will be a positive integer within the range specified.
